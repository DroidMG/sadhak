### 🕉️ **Sadhak: A Guided Sadhana Planner**

"Sadhak" is a comprehensive web application designed to help you plan, organize, and follow a complete spiritual discipline (*sādhanā*) from its inception to its conclusion. It acts as a personal guide for complex, multi-day spiritual observances.

Unlike a simple tracker, this app allows you to structure your entire practice beforehand. You can enter key details such as:

*   The name or type of the Sadhana
*   The start date and total duration (number of days)
*   Specific goals, like the total number of mantra japa (chants)
*   Details for rituals like Yagya (fire ceremony)

The app then guides you through all the necessary steps of the observance, ensuring you stay on track all the way to the concluding rituals, such as the Brahman Bhojan (feeding of Brahmins). It is the perfect tool for anyone undertaking a structured, long-term spiritual practice.

### 📲 **How to Install the "Sadhak" PWA**

The installation process remains the same. Here’s how you can add it to your devices to use it like a native app!

#### **💻 On a Desktop or Laptop (Windows, macOS, Linux)**

1.  **Open the link** in a browser like Chrome or Edge: `https://droidmg.github.io/sadhak`
2.  **Look for the Install icon** 🖥️ in the address bar (top right). It looks like a small screen with a down arrow.
3.  **Click "Install"** in the confirmation pop-up.
4.  **All done!** 🎉 The app will now be in your Start Menu or Launchpad, ready to guide your Sadhana.

#### **📱 On a Mobile Device**

**Android (using Google Chrome)**

1.  **Launch Chrome** and navigate to `https://droidmg.github.io/sadhak`.
2.  **Tap the three-dot menu** ⋮ in the top-right corner.
3.  **Select "Install app"** or "Add to Home screen".
4.  **Confirm by tapping "Install"** in the pop-up.
5.  **You're set!** ✅ The "Sadhak" app icon will appear on your home screen.

**iOS (iPhone/iPad using Safari)**

1.  **Open Safari** and go to `https://droidmg.github.io/sadhak`.
2.  **Tap the Share icon** 📤 at the bottom of the screen.
3.  **Scroll down** and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4.  **Name the app** (it should default to "Sadhak") and tap **"Add"**.
5.  **Enjoy!** 🙏 The app is now on your home screen, ready to assist your spiritual journey.
